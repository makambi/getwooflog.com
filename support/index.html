Common questions

How do I reset my data?

Open WoofLog → Settings → Delete All Data. This removes all pets, log entries, medication and vaccine records, and photos — from your devices and from the app's storage in your iCloud.

How do I add a second pet?

Multi-pet support is planned for a future release. The current version supports a single dog per device.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email support@getwooflog.com with as much detail as you can share — device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

How do I share my dog's record with someone?

Open the Profile tab, tap Sharing, then Share your dog's profile. Send the invite to a partner, family member, or sitter — once they accept, you both see the same record, and every entry shows who logged it. Both of you need to be signed into iCloud. The owner can stop sharing at any time.

How is my data handled?

Your dog's record is stored privately in your own iCloud and synced across your devices. It is shared only with people you explicitly invite. WoofLog operates no servers and cannot access your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
