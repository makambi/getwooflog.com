Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-17

Who we are

WoofLog is developed by Vitalii Nechypor, operating as an individual based in the Netherlands. For questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact support@getwooflog.com.

What data WoofLog processes

WoofLog is a local-first iOS app for logging your dog's health. All data you enter — pet profile information, health log entries, medication records, photos — is stored only on your device. Your dog's records are never transmitted to our servers. We do not operate servers that receive your dog's health data.

The app does not:

  • Collect your email, name, or other personal identifiers
  • Transmit your dog's records, notes, or photos off your device
  • Integrate advertising SDKs, ad measurement, or cross-app tracking
  • Share data with any third party for advertising or data-broker use

Anonymous product analytics (opt-in)

If you opt in, WoofLog sends anonymous product usage events to PostHog Cloud EU (https://eu.i.posthog.com). We use them to understand which parts of the app are useful and where it falls short. Analytics is off by default; you can turn it on or off any time in Profile → Share Anonymous Usage Data, and declining does not lock any feature.

What we send when you opt in:

  • Which loggers you open and save
  • Whether you add medication, log doses, or log weight
  • How often you open History and how often you tap into a saved record
  • The number of photos attached to a saved entry (a count, not the photos themselves)

What we never send:

  • Your dog's name, breed, birthday, weight value, notes, or any health record content
  • Your name, email, phone, address, or any identifier you can be looked up by
  • Photo contents, filenames, EXIF, or any media metadata
  • Your precise location

Each event carries an anonymous identifier generated by the analytics SDK on your device. It is not linked to your Apple ID, email, or any account. Using "Delete All Data" in Profile resets that identifier and clears any queued events on your device.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Because WoofLog stores your dog's records only on your device, there is no personal data for which we act as a controller under the GDPR in connection with the records themselves.

Two narrow exceptions:

  • Anonymous product analytics (only if you opt in). The legal basis is your explicit consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Email support. If you contact us at support@getwooflog.com, the content of your email — including your email address and whatever you write — reaches our inbox. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to your support request. We retain support emails only for as long as needed to resolve the issue and for a reasonable period afterward in case you follow up.

Your rights under GDPR

Because we do not store your dog's records on our servers, we cannot export or delete app data on your behalf. You have full control from within the app:

  • Access: all your data is visible in the app
  • Erasure: use "Delete All Data" in Settings, or uninstall the app
  • Portability: data export is planned for a future release
  • Objection: uninstall the app to stop all processing
  • Withdraw consent to anonymous product analytics any time in Profile → Share Anonymous Usage Data; new events stop immediately. Because the analytics events are anonymous, we cannot retrieve or delete your individual analytics history on request — but you can stop further collection at any time.

For email support correspondence, you may request deletion of your emails from our inbox at any time by writing to support@getwooflog.com.

Children's privacy

WoofLog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be announced in the app's "What's New" on the App Store.

Contact

For any privacy questions: support@getwooflog.com. Email support is the only channel through which any personal data reaches the operator, and is retained only for the duration of the support interaction plus a reasonable follow-up window.
