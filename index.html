Log your dog's health.
See the history.

A focused iPhone app for dog owners who want to track symptoms, medication, and the daily picture of how their dog is doing — without the noise.

WoofLog is a health record for your dog. You log what you notice — a soft stool, a skin flare-up, a missed meal — and it stays there, ready for when your vet asks "how long has this been happening?" The record is stored privately in your own iCloud, syncs across your devices, and is shared only with the people you invite.

Seven focused loggers

Skin & coat, ears, eyes, stool, vomiting, urinary, and mobility — plus free-form notes. Each one captures the signals that actually matter without turning logging into homework.

Medications & vaccines

Log each dose as you give it, and see recent doses and what's due today at a glance. Vaccines and routine care live right alongside medications, all in one place.

Weight tracking

Log your dog's weight and watch the trend over time — handy for a growing puppy, a diet change, or something your vet is keeping an eye on.

Your dog's history

Everything you log lands in one place, organized by date. Look back any time to see how things have changed, and walk into the vet with the full picture.

Share with the people who help

Invite a partner, a family member, or a sitter to a dog's record. Everyone sees the same up-to-date history, and every entry shows who logged it.

Private by design

No sign-ups and no separate account — just your Apple ID. Your dog's record is stored privately in your own iCloud and shared only with the people you invite. Optional anonymous product analytics are off by default.

Built by a dog owner, for his dog

WoofLog started as something I needed myself. I wanted a way to keep track of the small things — the soft stool, the skipped meal, the slightly-off gait — so that when the big things came up, I had more than memory to go on. This is the app I wished existed.
